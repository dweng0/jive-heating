Where it's going

Prototype with simulated readings. The house is modelled from your photos, seen from the garden. A new reading arrives every 10 seconds and the flow eases to it. The second row of buttons changes the weather; the evening and overnight examples change the time of day. Drag the house to turn it, pinch or scroll to zoom, and use the buttons under the picture to switch to the diagram. Pick an example above, or stop the readings to see the stale state.
